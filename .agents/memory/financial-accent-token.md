---
name: Financial accent separation
description: Keep monetary values visually distinct from the Season 2 brand accent and nonfinancial green indicators.
---

Financial values use a dedicated lime token (`--money`, currently `#B6F23A`) rather than the Season 2 brand accent or the general `--acid` token.

**Why:** `--acid` also marks nonfinancial activity and positive indicators, while Season 2 green is a brand color. Reusing either for money makes the visual meanings overlap.

**How to apply:** Use the money token for income values and charts. Keep exercise/activity uses of `--acid` separate, and use neutral colors for expenses rather than reusing the Season 2 brand accent.
