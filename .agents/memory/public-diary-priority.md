The user wants the public diary restored in place of statistics, with the individual days directly accessible rather than hidden behind an aggregate dashboard.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested: “ripristinassi la pagina diario e la sostituissi al posto di statistiche,” after reporting that the days appeared to have disappeared.

**How to apply:** Preserve the diary as the primary navigation destination for this content. Do not replace its visible day list with statistics or require a statistics tab to reach existing days. Changes to presentation must not alter saved diary data.
